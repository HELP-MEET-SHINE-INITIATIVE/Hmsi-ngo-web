# HMSI Sensitive Media Inquiry Workshop: Slides Outline

## Cover
- **Main Title:** Handling Sensitive Media Inquiries Safely
- **Subtitle:** Internal Staff & Volunteer Workshop Guide — Help Meet Shine Initiative (HMSI)
- **Presenter:** HMSI Communications & Governance Team
- **Date:** August 2026

## Slide 1: Workshop Purpose & Core Principle
- **Title:** Protect People Before Reputation
- **Key Message:** Be helpful without guessing; refer sensitive inquiries immediately to the designated spokesperson.
- **Key Points:**
  - Media inquiries are normal accountability opportunities, not default hostility.
  - Operational discipline protects beneficiaries, staff, and donors.
  - Primary contact channel: contact@hmsi.org.ng.

## Slide 2: The Five Universal Response Rules
- **Title:** Five Rules Every Team Member Must Follow
- **Key Points:**
  - Stop & Identify: Outlet, reporter, question, deadline.
  - Do Not Improvise: Never guess figures or promise answers.
  - Protect People: Zero disclosure of private/safeguarding data.
  - Route Correctly: Designated spokesperson handles the record.
  - Preserve Records: Save original links/emails without altering.

## Slide 3: Decision Rights & Spokesperson Boundaries
- **Title:** Clear Roles Prevent Conflicting Messages
- **Key Points:**
  - Staff / Volunteers: Take details, use holding line, route immediately.
  - Designated Spokesperson: Only authorized voice for on-record statements.
  - Safeguarding / Privacy Lead: Controls sensitive and referral files.
  - Leadership / Trustees: Approve Level 3 and 4 public communications.

## Slide 4: The 5-Minute Safe Response Protocol
- **Title:** What To Do in the First Five Minutes
- **Key Points:**
  - Step 1: Stay calm and courteous.
  - Step 2: Capture reporter, outlet, topic, and deadline.
  - Step 3: Deliver standard holding response politely.
  - Step 4: Route via contact@hmsi.org.ng immediately.
  - Step 5: Preserve raw evidence without editing.

## Slide 5: Data Discipline: Targets vs. Completed Impact
- **Title:** Distinguish Goals, Receipts, and Reconciled Data
- **Key Points:**
  - Campaign Targets: e.g., ₦5M goal is not ₦5M spent.
  - Family Goals: e.g., "Food packs for 30 families" is an objective, not completed delivery until confirmed.
  - Organization-Reported: Label unreconciled figures clearly.
  - No Superlatives: Avoid "best," "leading," or "life-changing" unless externally verified.

## Slide 6: Safeguarding & Beneficiary Privacy First
- **Title:** Never Compromise Vulnerable People
- **Key Points:**
  - Service Consent ≠ Media Consent: Separate, informed, voluntary consent required.
  - Absolute Protection: Zero disclosure of child names, addresses, medical files, or bank details.
  - Emergency First: Immediate physical danger requires emergency services, not media relations.
  - Dignity in Imagery: Non-identifying visuals preferred.

## Slide 7: RAG Triage & Escalation Thresholds
- **Title:** Triage Rapidly from Green to Red
- **Key Points:**
  - Green (Low): Routine questions, neutral listings → standard referral.
  - Amber (Medium): Disputed figures, donor concerns, tight deadlines → same-day triage.
  - Red (High): Safeguarding, fraud, data breach, legal threats → immediate Trustee escalation.

## Slide 8: Interactive Scenario Exercises
- **Title:** Practice Common Media Scenarios
- **Key Points:**
  - Scenario A (Medical Campaign Child): Refuse identity disclosure; route safely.
  - Scenario B (Missing Food Distribution List): State facts are under review; avoid guessing.
  - Scenario C (Pre-Publication Approval Demand): Correct demonstrable factual errors only.

## Slide 9: Post-Incident Review & Staff Sign-Off
- **Title:** Continuous Learning & Team Commitment
- **Key Points:**
  - 7-Day Review: Analyze facts, timelines, and systems improvements.
  - Zero Retaliation: Whistleblowers and good-faith reporters are protected.
  - Staff Checklist: 10-point annual training sign-off commitment.
