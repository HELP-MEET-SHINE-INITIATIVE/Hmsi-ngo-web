First-Month Pulse Check

Listen early. Respond proportionately. Protect people and information.

Purpose: Use this check to identify early friction, support needs, and trust risks. It is not a performance score, attendance tracker, or requirement to remain active.

When in doubt

A single low score normally creates a support opportunity, not an automatic escalation.

  1. Check for immediate safety or confidentiality risk.
  2. Protect the task and assign an owner.
  3. Respond within the agreed service level.
  4. Verify whether the trigger resolved.

Never record here

Do not put passwords, tokens, private beneficiary details, medical details, raw safeguarding disclosures, or sensitive case information in the pulse or team dashboard.

Use the designated confidential route for urgent safety, safeguarding, privacy, security, harassment, or wellbeing concerns.
