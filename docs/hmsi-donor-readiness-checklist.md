# HMSI donor-readiness checklist

## Purpose

This checklist translates the 2026 **HMSI Professional Institutional Profile & Donor Readiness Document** into practical website and institutional-readiness actions. It is an operational checklist, not legal advice, an audit opinion, a statutory filing, or independent verification.

## Standards alignment

The website uses the 2024 Core Humanitarian Standard as a practical accountability reference for dignity, participation, do-no-harm, feedback and complaints, learning, competent people, and ethical resource management. WCAG 2.2 is the accessibility baseline for text alternatives, keyboard access, focus visibility, contrast, reflow, forms, and target sizes. This is an implementation baseline and not a certification claim.

## Public-claims register

The profile requires evidence-based treatment of high-impact public claims. Until supporting records are available, these figures should be labelled as **HMSI-reported**, include a reporting period where possible, and should not be presented as independently audited facts.

| Claim | Minimum supporting evidence to retain |
|---|---|
| `7 meals per US$1 donated` | Calculation methodology, date or period, programme location, number of meals, expenditure, funding source, and supporting financial records |
| `100+ personnel` | Definition of personnel category, such as employees, volunteers, consultants, field personnel, or programme workers, plus reporting period |
| `US$100,000+ annual income` | Financial records, reporting period, currency basis, and an approved organizational financial statement |
| `MEA Award for Most Productive NGO (2022)` | Certificate, official announcement, awarding organization, category, year, and supporting documentation |

## Partnership proposal framework

Each major institutional proposal should clearly define the problem, target population, geographic location, proposed intervention, expected outputs, expected outcomes, implementation period, budget, safeguarding arrangements, monitoring and evaluation plan, reporting commitments, and sustainability strategy. Proposals should distinguish planned activities from achieved results and should link each outcome claim to an evidence source.

HMSI’s intended institutional audiences include international NGOs, foundations, corporate organizations, government institutions, development agencies, philanthropic organizations, community organizations, individual donors, volunteers, and community partners. Partnership conversations should begin through [`/contact`](../app/contact/ContactForm.tsx).

## Website controls implemented

The website audit corrected the homepage’s stale **2018** incorporation label to **2019**, replaced unsupported headline figures and percentage claims with supplied institutional context, removed the conflicting **Lagos, Nigeria** location from shared contact surfaces, and added direct homepage navigation to Field Stories, Partner with us, and Transparency. The About page now reflects the supplied mission, vision, and core values. The Trust Center and Partnerships page explain what evidence donors can request without pretending that missing reports or policies already exist.

## Remaining HMSI actions

HMSI should provide or approve the following before public publication as downloadable institutional evidence: redacted CAC and governing documents, an approved FIRS record or tax-compliance document where appropriate, annual reports, audited or reviewed financial summaries, programme and impact reports, safeguarding and child-protection policy, anti-fraud policy, conflict-of-interest policy, complaints procedure, data-protection/privacy policy, MEL records, award evidence, partnership references or agreements, and consented programme photographs. The raw uploaded scans should remain restricted because they contain personal data and unrelated private-company records.

The Terms & conditions page remains a working website draft and should be reviewed by qualified Nigerian legal counsel before being relied upon as a formal agreement. Public claims, award statements, financial figures, and programme results should also be reviewed by authorized HMSI leadership.
