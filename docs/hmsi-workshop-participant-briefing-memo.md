## 1. Welcome & Purpose of This Workshop

Welcome to today’s operational training workshop on **Handling Sensitive Media Inquiries Safely**. 

In the course of our community, humanitarian, and fundraising work at Help Meet Shine Initiative (HMSI), we regularly interact with journalists, community reporters, bloggers, donors, and members of the public. Media inquiries are a completely normal and healthy part of NGO accountability. They are not hostile attacks, and they should never cause panic.

However, an uncoordinated or hurried response can inadvertently expose vulnerable children, compromise beneficiary medical privacy, create legal liabilities, or spread unverified metrics. 

This interactive workshop is designed to provide you with practical, hands-on muscle memory so that you feel confident, calm, and fully supported whenever you encounter an unexpected media inquiry—whether over the phone, in the field, or via social media.

---

## 2. Core Learning Objectives

By the end of today’s role-playing simulations, every participant will be able to:

1. **Apply the 5-Minute Safe Response Protocol:** Seamlessly execute the 5-minute action flow (Pause $\rightarrow$ Capture $\rightarrow$ Hold $\rightarrow$ Route $\rightarrow$ Preserve).
2. **Prioritize People Over Reputation:** Protect child identities, medical conditions, and vulnerable case records without exception.
3. **Resist Coercion & Deadline Traps:** Confidently decline "off-the-record" requests and resist manufactured urgency.
4. **Distinguish Goals from Reconciled Data:** Explain campaign targets accurately without guessing unverified delivery numbers.
5. **Route & Escalate Correctly:** Direct all on-record questions to `contact@hmsi.org.ng` and immediately escalate Red-level safety/fraud incidents.

---

## 3. How the Role-Playing Exercises Work

You will be paired with a colleague to conduct three realistic, 5-minute simulation exercises:

| Step | Time | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Role Assignment** | 1 Min | One participant plays the **Journalist / Inquirer**; one plays the **HMSI Team Member**. |
| **2. Active Simulation** | 5 Mins | The scenario runs uninterrupted on a 5-minute timer. The "Journalist" applies realistic pressure; the "Staff Member" executes the protocol. |
| **3. Peer Review & Scoring** | 3 Mins | Using the 10-Point Evaluation Rubric, pairs provide constructive feedback to each other. |
| **4. Role Swap & Next Scenario** | 8 Mins | Switch roles and proceed to the next simulation scenario. |
| **5. Group Plenary Debrief** | 10 Mins | Facilitator-led group discussion on key takeaways, difficult moments, and lessons learned. |

---

## 4. Workshop Ground Rules & Expectations

To ensure a productive, safe, and engaging environment, all participants must observe these four rules:

1. **Stay in Character:** Treat the exercise as a real-world encounter. Do not break character until the 5-minute timer sounds.
2. **Realistic Pressure, Mutual Respect:** The "Journalist" should simulate real-world tactics (urgency, skepticism, deadline pressure, flattery), but must remain professional and courteous.
3. **Use Your Reference Materials:** Keep your **1-Page Pocket Reference Card** in front of you. You are encouraged to refer to it during the simulation!
4. **Mistakes Are Welcome:** This is a safe practice laboratory. Making a mistake during the simulation is the best way to learn before handling real-world inquiries.

---

## 5. Your Essential Tool: The Core Holding Script

When in doubt, remember the universal verbal response approved for all HMSI staff and volunteers:

> ### Approved Verbal Holding Script
> *"Thank you for contacting Help Meet Shine Initiative. I am not authorized to provide on-record statements, but I will refer your inquiry to our designated spokesperson for verified information. Please send your deadline and specific questions to **contact@hmsi.org.ng**."*

---

## 6. What NEVER to Disclose Under Any Circumstance

During today's exercises—and in all real-world interactions—the following are **strictly prohibited**:

- $\times$ **Never share a child's full name, school, home address, or medical diagnosis.**
- $\times$ **Never share beneficiary case files, personal phone numbers, or private bank details.**
- $\times$ **Never guess or invent numbers** (e.g., claiming food packs were delivered before field receipts are reconciled).
- $\times$ **Never agree to speak "off the record" or give personal opinions on organizational matters.**
- $\times$ **Never argue with journalists or critics from your personal social media accounts.**

---

## 7. Psychological Safety & Non-Retaliation Commitment

HMSI operates under a strict **Zero Retaliation Policy**. 

Today's exercises are designed for professional development, empowerment, and team solidarity—not performance grading or disciplinary appraisal. Furthermore, in real-world operations, any staff member or volunteer who flags a safeguarding concern, reports an error, or refers a sensitive media inquiry in good faith will always be supported and protected by leadership and the Board of Trustees.
