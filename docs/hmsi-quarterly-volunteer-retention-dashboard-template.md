**Last updated:** [Date]

> **Purpose:** Monitor whether the HMSI volunteer experience is clear, safe, respectful, meaningful, and sustainable over the next quarter. This dashboard is a service-improvement instrument, not a ranking system for volunteers or teams.

---

## 1. Dashboard Use and Evidence Rules

This template should be populated only with approved operational and survey data. Do not invent values to fill empty cells. Every percentage must show its numerator and denominator, and every comparison must state the reporting period used.

The dashboard should help team leads answer five questions:

1. Are volunteers entering and completing onboarding successfully?
2. Can volunteers understand and complete suitable activities?
3. Are support, portal, communication, and safeguarding routes working?
4. Do volunteers want and feel able to continue participating?
5. Which practical improvements should HMSI prioritize next?

A dashboard result is an indicator, not a complete explanation. A lower continuation rate may reflect seasonal availability, programme pauses, transport conditions, family responsibilities, portal access, unclear roles, or safety concerns. Use comments, support records, and authorized follow-up to understand the context.

## 3. Metric Definitions and Formulas

Use these definitions consistently across the quarter. If HMSI changes a definition, record the change in the data dictionary and do not compare the revised measure directly with earlier periods without a note.

| Metric | Definition | Formula | Recommended cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eligible volunteers at start** | Volunteers who were approved or active at the beginning of the measurement period and were eligible for participation. | Count of records meeting the approved eligibility rule. | Monthly and quarterly |
| **Active volunteer** | A volunteer who completed at least one approved activity, task update, training step, or other defined contribution during the period. | Count of unique volunteers meeting the activity rule. | Weekly and monthly |
| **Quarterly active-retention rate** | The share of eligible volunteers at the start who remained active at the end or completed the approved continuation definition. | Active eligible volunteers at end ÷ eligible volunteers at start × 100. | Monthly and quarterly |
| **Onboarding completion rate** | The share of new volunteers completing the required onboarding steps. | Completed onboarding records ÷ new eligible volunteers × 100. | Weekly and monthly |
| **First-task activation rate** | The share of new volunteers accepting or beginning a suitable first task within the approved activation window. | Volunteers with first-task activation ÷ new eligible volunteers × 100. | Weekly and monthly |
| **Task completion rate** | The share of eligible assigned tasks completed with the required outcome and proof. | Completed eligible tasks ÷ eligible tasks due in period × 100. | Weekly and monthly |
| **Blocker-resolution time** | Time from a recorded blocker to an authorized resolution or documented next step. | Median or average elapsed time; state which one is used. | Weekly and monthly |
| **Survey response rate** | The share of invited volunteers who submitted the relevant survey. | Completed responses ÷ invited volunteers × 100. | Per survey cycle |
| **Positive experience signal** | The share selecting 4 or 5 on a defined five-point survey item or approved composite. | Positive responses ÷ valid responses to item × 100. | Monthly and quarterly |
| **Continuation intention** | The share selecting a continuation option such as continue, increase, or suitable next activity. | Positive continuation responses ÷ valid continuation responses × 100. | Monthly and quarterly |
| **Respectful exit rate** | The share of volunteers who paused or left with a recorded, non-punitive exit or pause path. | Supported exits or pauses ÷ recorded exits or pauses × 100. | Monthly and quarterly |
| **Recognition coverage** | The share of completed eligible contributions receiving a timely, appropriate acknowledgement. | Recognized eligible contributions ÷ eligible contributions × 100. | Monthly |

### Recommended exclusions

Document exclusions before calculating a metric. Possible exclusions include duplicate records, test accounts, records created after the measurement period, volunteers who explicitly paused before the period began, tasks cancelled by HMSI, and records lacking enough information to meet the defined denominator. Do not exclude difficult responses simply because they lower a result.

### Suggested retention actions

| Problem pattern | Suitable action examples |
|---|---|
| Volunteers do not understand their role | Rewrite role descriptions, add a first-week orientation example, and confirm approval boundaries. |
| Volunteers do not know what to do next | Add a named contact, clear task cards, and a visible next-step message. |
| Portal or proof workflow causes friction | Add a safe proof-link example, improve mobile guidance, and test the flow with a new cohort. |
| Volunteers feel disconnected from impact | Provide approved contribution summaries, feedback from programme leads, and responsible recognition. |
| Volunteers are overextended | Offer smaller tasks, realistic due dates, pause options, and explicit capacity checks. |
| Volunteers report uncertainty about safety or privacy | Reinforce consent, minimum-necessary information, confidential reporting, and escalation guidance. |

## 10. Monthly Review Agenda

A monthly review should be short, evidence-based, and action-oriented.

| Time | Agenda item | Decision or output |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 minutes | Confirm period, definitions, coverage, and data quality | Agreed evidence boundary |
| 10 minutes | Review quarterly summary and trend view | Top strengths and concerns |
| 10 minutes | Review survey themes and operational signals | Root-cause hypotheses and follow-up needs |
| 5 minutes | Review safety, privacy, security, and wellbeing referrals | Correct confidential routing |
| 10 minutes | Update the action register | Owners, dates, measures, and status |
| 5 minutes | Confirm volunteer communication and next review | Close-the-loop plan |

**Meeting outcome:**

> We will preserve **[strength]**, improve **[priority issue]**, test **[change]**, and review **[measure]** by **[date]**.

## 11. Quarterly Review and Learning Questions

At the end of the quarter, ask whether the organization improved the experience rather than simply increased activity.

1. Did new volunteers understand the HMSI mission, values, role boundaries, and support routes more clearly?
2. Did volunteers reach a suitable first task with less friction?
3. Did portal, task, proof, or communication improvements reduce avoidable blockers?
4. Did volunteers report a stronger sense of dignity, inclusion, connection, and purpose?
5. Did HMSI make it safe and respectful for volunteers to pause, reduce involvement, or raise concerns?
6. Which action should be retained, expanded, redesigned, or stopped?
7. What should the next survey or onboarding cohort test?

## 13. Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On track** | The measure is being collected consistently and the related action is progressing. |
| **Needs attention** | The result or process shows a meaningful concern that has an identified owner. |
| **Blocked** | Progress requires a decision, resource, access change, or confidential referral. |
| **Monitoring** | The evidence is not yet strong enough for a major action, but the signal is being tracked. |
| **Complete** | The action was implemented and its verification measure was reviewed. |
| **Deferred** | The action is intentionally postponed with a documented reason and review date. |
