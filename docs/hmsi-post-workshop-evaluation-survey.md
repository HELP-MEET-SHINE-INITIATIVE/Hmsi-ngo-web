## Section 5: Administrative Review & Scoring Guide (For Management / Facilitators)

### Quantitative Performance Benchmarks
To evaluate overall workshop effectiveness, the Communications Lead and Trustees will analyze survey results against these quality targets:

| Metric | Target Benchmark | Corrective Action if Below Benchmark |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Average Confidence Gain** | $\ge +1.5$ point increase across all competencies | Schedule follow-up micro-drills within 30 days. |
| **High Confidence Rate** | $\ge 85\%$ of participants score $\ge 4$ on Post-Training Competency 4 (Data Protection) | Conduct mandatory 1-on-1 safeguarding review. |
| **Protocol Mastery** | $\ge 90\%$ correct responses on Section 3 Scenario Knowledge Check | Clarify holding scripts in monthly team briefing. |
| **Material Satisfaction** | $\ge 4.2 / 5.0$ average rating on Pocket Reference Card & Role-Playing | Refine simulation scripts and field card design. |

---

### Survey Analysis & Reporting Workflow
1. **Data Collection:** Facilitator collates completed survey forms within 48 hours post-workshop.
2. **Gap Analysis:** Highlight any question or competency where $\ge 15\%$ of participants scored $\le 3$.
3. **Executive Summary Memo:** Communications Lead submits a 1-page workshop outcome report to the Board of Trustees noting aggregate scores, identified risks, and scheduled refresher dates.
4. **Continuous Tool Updates:** Any recurring confusion regarding numbers, definitions, or escalation contacts will be directly incorporated into the next revision of the *1-Page Pocket Reference Card*.
