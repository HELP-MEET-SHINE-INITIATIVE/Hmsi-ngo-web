# HMSI Sensitive Media Inquiry — 1-Page Pocket Reference Card

**Official Internal Quick-Action Guide for All Staff, Workers & Volunteers**  
*Help Meet Shine Initiative (HMSI) — August 2026 Edition*

---

## Core Principle & 5 Universal Rules

> **"Be helpful without guessing, protect people before reputation, and refer all on-record questions to the designated spokesperson."**  
> *Media inquiries are standard accountability, not hostility.*

| 5 Universal Response Rules | Operational Mandate |
|---|---|
| **1. Stop & Identify** | Note journalist's full name, media outlet, specific questions, and publication deadline. |
| **2. Do Not Improvise** | Never guess figures, promise coverage results, or speak "off the record." |
| **3. Protect Data** | Zero disclosure of child identities, medical files, donor details, or private case records. |
| **4. Route Correctly** | Refer all on-record inquiries through the official email: `contact@hmsi.org.ng`. |
| **5. Save Records** | Preserve original message text, timestamps, and screenshots without altering evidence. |

---

## 1. The 5-Minute Safe Response Protocol

| Minute | Stage | Action & Verbal Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Min 1** | **Pause & Greet** | Stay calm and courteous. Do not offer immediate off-the-cuff statements or defensive comments. |
| **Min 2** | **Capture Facts** | Note reporter name, outlet/platform, specific questions, contact email/phone, and deadline. |
| **Min 3** | **Deliver Holding Line** | Politely state that you are not the spokesperson and provide the official centralized routing email. |
| **Min 4–5** | **Route & Save** | Forward notes to the Communications Lead; save screenshots and timestamps to the incident log. |

### Exact Phone / Verbal Holding Script

> *"Thank you for contacting HMSI. I am not authorized to give on-record comments, but I will refer your inquiry to our designated spokesperson for verified information. Please send your deadline and specific questions to **contact@hmsi.org.ng**."*

## 2. RAG Triage & Escalation Thresholds

| Severity Level | Trigger Criteria & Examples | Response Target | Incident Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GREEN (Low)** | Routine program questions, neutral directory listings, event invitations, public website clarifications. | 1–2 business days (Standard response) | Communications Team / Spokesperson |
| **AMBER (Medium)** | Disputed metrics/figures, donor/partner concerns, material news criticism, tight deadlines (< 24 hrs). | Triage < 4 hours; Holding line while verifying records | Spokesperson & Program Lead |
| **RED (High / Crisis)** | **Safeguarding / child abuse claims**, fraud / fund misuse, data breach, extortion/scam, legal/regulator demand. | **Immediate escalation**; Zero public debate; Safety/legal priority | **Trustees, President & Legal Lead** |

---

## 3. Strict Prohibitions & Key Contacts

### What NEVER to Disclose (Strictly Prohibited)
- **No Child Data:** Never disclose minor names, schools, or home locations—even if on a fundraising page.
- **No Medical / Case Files:** Medical conditions, hospital data, and private case files remain strictly confidential.
- **No Personal Accounts:** Never argue with journalists or defend HMSI from personal social media accounts.
- **No Guessing Metrics:** Distinguish campaign targets (e.g. "₦5M goal") from audited expenditure.

### Key Emergency & Escalation Contacts
- **Official Media & Inquiries:** `contact@hmsi.org.ng`
- **Safeguarding & Protection Concerns:** Mark email subject `[SAFEGUARDING]`
- **Data Privacy & Breach Alerts:** Mark email subject `[PRIVACY]`
- **Immediate Physical Danger or Abuse:** Alert local emergency services immediately!
