# HMSI Volunteer Retention Implementation Plan
## Next-Quarter Timeline and Checklist

**Organization:** Help Meet Shine Initiative (HMSI)  
**Planning period:** [Quarter and year]  
**Executive sponsor:** [Name / role]  
**Implementation coordinator:** [Name / role]  
**Plan status:** Draft for approval

> **Plan objective:** Improve the quality and sustainability of volunteer participation by making the first month clearer, support more reliable, and safety, dignity, inclusion, privacy, and wellbeing more visible in everyday operations.

This plan implements the three strategic priorities in the executive memo: **clearer onboarding and role pathways**, **a reliable support and feedback loop**, and **safe, inclusive, sustainable participation**. It is designed to be populated with verified operational data and adjusted to HMSI’s actual capacity. It does not assume specific survey results or promise a fixed retention outcome before baseline data is available.

## 2. Implementation Principles

The quarter should begin with definitions and safe operating boundaries, not with public targets. Every measure must have an owner, denominator, reporting period, and context note. Safety, safeguarding, privacy, security, and wellbeing matters must use their designated confidential routes and must not be collapsed into a general retention score.

The team should make improvements with volunteers rather than merely for volunteers. Where practical, test revised onboarding, portal guidance, task cards, and support routes with a small group before expanding them. A volunteer may pause or leave for legitimate personal reasons; respectful pause and exit options are part of a healthy retention strategy.

## 8. Risk Register

| Risk | Early warning signal | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding changes are launched without enough testing | Repeated role or portal confusion | Pilot with a small cohort and fix critical issues before rollout. | [Role] | Open |
| Retention becomes a pressure target | Volunteers hesitate to pause or report concerns | Track sustainable participation and supported pauses; reinforce non-punitive language. | [Role] | Open |
| Survey results are overinterpreted | Broad claims based on small or incomplete response sets | Show denominators, limitations, and response coverage. | [Role] | Open |
| Confidential information enters the dashboard | Raw disclosures or identifiable comments appear in summaries | Restrict access, suppress small cells, and route sensitive matters separately. | [Role] | Open |
| Actions are agreed without capacity | Multiple overdue owners or unresolved blockers | Limit commitments and confirm resources at each gate. | [Role] | Open |
| Recognition becomes competitive | Volunteers feel pressured or publicly ranked | Use specific, truthful, dignity-preserving acknowledgement. | [Role] | Open |
| Portal or support route changes cause new confusion | Increased access or blocker reports after rollout | Version changes, monitor closely, and provide a clear rollback or correction path. | [Role] | Open |

## 9. Weekly Status Checklist

### Weeks 1–4: Define and test

- [ ] Owners, definitions, data access, and confidential routes confirmed.
- [ ] Onboarding, role, task, support, safety, privacy, and pause-path audits completed.
- [ ] Draft materials reviewed by the relevant authorized owners.
- [ ] Safe pilot completed with issues and decisions recorded.
- [ ] Rollout decision approved.

### Weeks 5–8: Roll out and learn

- [ ] Approved changes delivered to the next eligible cohort.
- [ ] First-task check-ins and support route are operating.
- [ ] Safety and privacy orientation is delivered.
- [ ] Monthly dashboard is populated with verified values.
- [ ] Mid-quarter action review and volunteer update completed.

### Weeks 9–12: Improve and close

- [ ] Recurring blockers and pathway-specific issues reviewed.
- [ ] Next one-month survey response set collected without fabricated values.
- [ ] Action register items closed, carried forward, or deferred with reasons.
- [ ] Quarterly summary and limitations reviewed by leadership.
- [ ] Next-quarter priorities, owners, and review date confirmed.
