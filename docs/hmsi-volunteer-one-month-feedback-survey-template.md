> Thank you for completing your first month with HMSI. Your feedback helps us improve the way we welcome, support, and work alongside volunteers. Please answer honestly. There are no “correct” answers, and constructive feedback will not affect your standing with HMSI.

## Survey Administration Notes

The survey should be sent near the end of the volunteer’s first month and should remain available for a reasonable response period. A reminder should be neutral and should not pressure volunteers to provide positive feedback. Results should be reviewed for patterns across onboarding, role clarity, portal usability, safeguarding awareness, inclusion, and continuation intent.

Aggregate findings may be used to improve training and portal design. Individual comments should be shared only with people who need them for a legitimate support, safeguarding, security, or operational purpose. Any allegation or urgent safety concern identified through the survey should be moved to the correct confidential reporting process rather than handled through general survey analysis.

The survey is intended to improve the volunteer experience. It is not a performance-scoring instrument and should not be used to punish volunteers for raising good-faith concerns.
